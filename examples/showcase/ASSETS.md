## Reproduce the small assets

The font is from Google Fonts revision
`b31870aff700ab7a1d74fa0c6887d95beb9e0037`. Its SHA-256 is
`3ae11c49db0455a3cc33e37d380f20fdb8c7f8b41dc07625c177e3d87a9d6ae6`.
The original photo's SHA-256 is
`6936f552123608df0552d3b3ddaaeabdff3ed233e669b04676ac8078d6c0aad6`.

Download the original photo from
[the pinned original file](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e5/Landscape_in_Salzburger_Land.jpg)
and reproduce the checked-in derivative with FFmpeg:

```bash
ffmpeg -i landscape-original.jpg -vf scale=1600:-1 -map_metadata -1 -q:v 3 landscape.jpg
```

Encoding bytes can differ by FFmpeg version; the source checksum identifies the
input. The original 4.3 MiB photo is not committed; the small derivative is bundled.

and downloaded source bytes, and fails on a changed/missing source or FFmpeg
error. FFmpeg encoder bytes can vary by version; the source selections and
processing commands remain reproducible.

Each showcase includes a `render.mp4`, `poster.jpg` and compact animated
`preview.gif` (silent). These are actual CPU renders of the accompanying Python projects
at 1280×720 and 30 fps. They may be viewed or downloaded directly on GitHub.

credit and source/license links when redistributing them. Other outputs use the
